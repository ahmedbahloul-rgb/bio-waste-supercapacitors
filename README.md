# 🇩🇿 NOYAU — Bio-Waste Supercapacitors

## From Algerian Agricultural Waste to Advanced Energy Storage

**NOYAU** is a research and innovation project focused on transforming abundant Algerian agricultural residues — particularly **date pits and olive pits** — into high-value activated carbon materials for advanced supercapacitor electrodes.

Our objective is to develop **sustainable, locally sourced and high-performance energy-storage materials**, while creating a pathway from agricultural waste valorization to industrial supercapacitor manufacturing.

---

## ⚡ Our Technology

We convert biomass-derived carbon precursors into porous activated carbon and engineer them into electrochemical electrodes for supercapacitor applications.

Our research combines:

* Activated carbon
* Electrochemistry
* Conducting polymers
* MnO₂-based materials
* Reduced graphene oxide
* Biomass valorization
* Supercapacitor electrode engineering

The project is being developed at **Université de M'sila, Algeria**.

---

## 📊 Key Research Results

Our laboratory research has demonstrated promising electrochemical performance:

| Parameter                          |                        Result |
| ---------------------------------- | ----------------------------: |
| Specific capacitance               |                 **528 F g⁻¹** |
| Specific energy                    |             **36.82 Wh kg⁻¹** |
| Capacitance retention              | **96.5% after 10,000 cycles** |
| Hybrid electrode capacitance       |               **582.6 F g⁻¹** |
| Hybrid electrode cycling retention |    **90% after 3,000 cycles** |

The results include binary and ternary carbon materials based on **date pits, olive pits and sugar**, as well as hybrid electrodes combining **olive-stone-derived activated carbon, polyaniline (PAni) and reduced graphene oxide (rGO)**.

> Performance values depend on electrode composition, electrolyte, cell configuration and testing conditions. Further validation at device and pilot scale is part of the development roadmap.

## 🌱 Why Agricultural Waste?

Algeria has significant agricultural resources, including dates and olives, which generate substantial quantities of biomass residues.

Instead of treating these residues as low-value waste, NOYAU aims to transform them into **functional carbon materials for energy storage**.

### Our approach

**Agricultural residues → Carbonization → Activation → Electrode engineering → Supercapacitor → Energy storage**

This approach offers several potential advantages:

* ♻️ Waste valorization
* 🌱 Sustainable carbon feedstock
* 🇩🇿 Local raw materials
* 💰 Potentially lower material costs
* 🔬 Advanced materials research
* ⚡ Energy-storage applications
* 🏭 Potential for local industrial production
